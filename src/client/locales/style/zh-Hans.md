# Simplified Chinese (简体中文)

- Address the player as 你, never 您.
- Game terms, from the game’s Simplified Chinese text: **大头菜** (counted in 棵: “100棵”), **铃钱**, **曹卖**, **Nook商店**, **豆狸** and **粒狸**, **岛**. Never write 芜菁, 贝尔, 黛西 or 狸猫商店.
- Unsold turnips **坏掉** (the game’s item is “坏掉的大头菜”).
- Pattern names, as players know them from Turnip Prophet: **波动型**, **大幅上涨**, **递减型**, **小幅上涨** (many players also say 三期型 and 四期型 for the spikes). A pattern is a **趋势**.
- Half-days: “星期一上午”, “星期一下午”; short labels “周一上午”; columns “上午”, “下午”; under the narrow chart columns “上”, “下”. A half-day is a **时段**.
- Times use the 24-hour clock: “8:00”, “22:00”, “中午”.
- “Profile” is **个人资料**; “device” is **设备**.
- Use full-width punctuation (，。：；！？（）) and “” for quotations. Within a sentence, no spaces between Chinese and numbers or Latin words, as in the game (“Nook商店”, “100棵”); a label and its value, as in chips, keep one.
