# Korean (한국어)

- Write in friendly 해요체. Avoid 당신; the player’s own things are 내 (“내 섬”, “내 가격”), as in most Korean apps, and the tag after the player’s name is 나.
- Game terms, from the game’s Korean text: **무** (counted in 개: “무 100개”), **벨**, **무파니**, **너굴상점**, **콩돌이** and **밤돌이**, **섬**. Never write 순무, 데이지 or Nook’s Cranny.
- Unsold turnips **썩어요** (the game’s item is “썩은 무”).
- Pattern names, as Korean players know them from Turnip Prophet: **파동형**, **큰 급등**, **감소**, **작은 급등**. A pattern is a **패턴**: “감소 패턴의 주였어요”.
- Half-days: “월요일 오전”, “월요일 오후”; short labels and columns “월 오전”, “오전”; under the narrow chart columns “AM”, “PM”. A half-day is a **시간대**.
- Particles like 은/는, 이/가 and 을/를 change with the word before them, which a placeholder hides. After a placeholder use particles that don’t change (의, 에, 에서, 보다, 까지) or a colon: “{name}의 섬”, “{when}에”, “{known}보다”. After fixed words like 벨 choose the right form as usual.
- Times: “오전 8시”, “오후 10시”, “정오”. Ranges use ~: “90~110벨”.
- Keep Korean word spacing; the app wraps lines between words.
