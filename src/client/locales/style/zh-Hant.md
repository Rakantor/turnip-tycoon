# Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)

- Write for players in Taiwan and Hong Kong, with Taiwan’s software vocabulary: 裝置, 連線, 設定, 儲存, 載入, 資料, 伺服器, 個人檔案 (profile). This is not a character conversion of the Simplified Chinese catalog.
- Address the player as 你, never 您.
- Game terms, from the game’s Traditional Chinese text: **大頭菜** (counted in 棵: “100棵”), **鈴錢**, **曹賣**, **Nook商店**, **豆狸** and **粒狸**, **島**. Never write 蕪菁, 貝爾, 黛西 or 狸貓商店.
- Unsold turnips **壞掉** (the game’s item is “壞掉的大頭菜”).
- Pattern names, as players know them from Turnip Prophet: **波型**, **三期型** (large spike), **遞減型**, **四期型** (small spike). A pattern is a **模型**.
- Half-days: “星期一上午”, “星期一下午”; short labels “週一上午”; columns “上午”, “下午”; under the narrow chart columns “上”, “下”. A half-day is a **時段**.
- Times use the 24-hour clock: “8:00”, “22:00”, “中午”.
- Use full-width punctuation (，。：；！？（）) and 「」 for quotations. Within a sentence, no spaces between Chinese and numbers or Latin words, as in the game (“Nook商店”, “100棵”); a label and its value, as in chips, keep one.
